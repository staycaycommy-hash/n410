# Changelog

All notable changes to this project.

## [0.1.0] — 2026-06-14

Initial public release. Cinematic one-pager for the No410 single-unit detached residence in Miri.

### Added
- Animated hero with Higgsfield-generated video background (clouds, wind, light), WebM + MP4 dual-source, static-image fallback.
- Preloader with bronze "410" fill animation and progress bar.
- Reveal-on-scroll, word-by-word tease, count-up stats (3,406.77 sq ft, 19 points).
- Five photoreal exterior story spreads with parallax.
- **The Design** — 5 architectural studies (1 high-res PDF perspective + 4 enhanced SketchUp angle views).
- **The Elevations** — all four sides from the architect's drawings, restyled to the brochure palette.
- **The Plans** — ground + first floor with tab switcher, room legends, and key metrics.
- Styled SVG **location map** with drive times to 5 destinations and "View on Google Maps" link.
- Closing block with price/completion/tenure, WhatsApp CTA, developer credit, Elite Estate Group authorised-agency mark.
- Scroll progress bar, fixed architectural frame labels, pulsing site pin.

### Known
- WhatsApp CTA uses placeholder number `60000000000`.
- Drive times are estimates calculated from coordinates, not live Google data.
